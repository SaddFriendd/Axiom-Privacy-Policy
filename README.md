# Privacy Policy

**Axiom**
Last updated: June 4, 2026

---

## 1. Introduction

Axiom ("we", "our", or "us") is an AI character chat application. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

By using Axiom, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

**Account Information**
When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from your Google account.

**Chat Data**
Messages you send within the app are stored in our database to enable conversation history and continuity across sessions.

**Usage Data**
We track the number of messages you send in order to enforce fair usage limits tied to your subscription plan.

**Profile Data**
Any profile information you choose to set (display name, email visibility preferences) is stored and associated with your account.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

- To provide and operate the Axiom service
- To authenticate your identity via Google Sign-In
- To store and display your conversation history
- To enforce message limits based on your subscription plan
- To display your profile information within the app

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

---

## 4. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a cloud database provider. Data is stored on servers and is protected by industry-standard security measures including Row Level Security (RLS), meaning users can only access their own data.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services

Axiom uses the following third-party services:

- **Google Sign-In** — for authentication
- **Supabase** — for database and backend services
- **Anthropic / AI providers** — to generate AI character responses

These services have their own privacy policies and handle data according to their respective terms.

---

## 6. Data Retention

Your account data, messages, and profile information are retained as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

Axiom is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

---

## 8. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your account and associated data
- Withdraw consent at any time by stopping use of the app

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
